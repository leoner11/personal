Privacy Policy

Personal · Effective 18 September 2026

Personal is a personal CRM app for iPhone, Android, Mac and Windows. It is developed by Leonard Davidson under the name MJCX Studio (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

The short version. Personal works entirely on your device, and nothing leaves it unless you create an account to sync. If you do, what you sync is sent over an encrypted connection and stored on our server so your devices can share it. We don’t show ads, we don’t use analytics or tracking, and we don’t sell or share your data. You can delete your account and everything synced to it from inside the app at any time.

Without an account

Everything you enter — people, notes, tasks and the rest — is stored only on your device. None of it is sent to us. Reminders are scheduled on your device too; we don’t use a push notification service.

With an account

Creating an account is optional, and is only needed to sync between your devices. When you have one, we store:

  • Your account: your email address, which is how you sign in, and your password. Passwords are stored only as a secure one-way hash, never in a form we can read. We don’t send marketing email.
  • What you sync: the people you add (such as names, companies, WhatsApp numbers, WeChat IDs, where and when you met, notes, and which occasions apply to them), occasions and occasion tags, projects, money entries, notes, logged contacts, meetings and tasks.
  • Signed-in devices: a sign-in token for each device, with a short label such as “iPhone” or “Mac” and when it was last used, so that signing out on one device does not sign out the others.
  • Technical information: our server records the IP address of requests in its logs, and briefly counts sign-in and sign-up attempts per network to block abuse.

How it’s protected

Everything sent between the app and our server travels over an encrypted connection (HTTPS), and the app refuses to sync over an unencrypted one. Sign-in tokens are stored in your device’s secure storage (Keychain on Apple devices, Keystore on Android) and only as a hash on our server. Access to the server is restricted, and we only access your data to keep the service running or when you ask us to.

What we don’t do

  • We don’t show ads.
  • We don’t use analytics, crash-reporting or tracking tools.
  • We don’t sell your information, or share it with anyone for their own purposes.

Your synced data is stored with our hosting provider, DigitalOcean, which runs the servers on our behalf. We would only disclose information if the law requires it.

Features that use other apps

  • WhatsApp and WeChat: when you choose to send a message, the app opens WhatsApp with the message filled in, or copies the message for WeChat. We don’t read your chats. Those apps’ own privacy policies apply to what you send there.
  • Calendar: when you tap “Add to calendar”, your device’s calendar opens a new event for you to confirm. The app never reads your calendar.
  • Notifications: reminders are created and shown on your device only.

Information about other people

Personal is for keeping track of the people you know, so much of what you store is about other people. Please only add information you are entitled to keep, and use it in line with the laws that apply to you.

How long we keep it

  • Your account and synced data are kept until you delete them.
  • Our server keeps daily backups for 14 days, so deleted data disappears from backups within 14 days.
  • Server logs containing IP addresses are kept for up to 90 days.
  • Sign-in and sign-up attempt counts are held in memory for at most an hour.

Deleting your data

You can delete your account in the app, under Account. This immediately removes your account, everything synced to it, and signs out your other devices. Data stored on a device stays on that device until you delete it or remove the app.

You can also email us to ask for a copy of your data, to correct it, or to delete it.

Children

Personal is not directed at children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page. Changes that affect how your synced data is handled will also be noted in the app’s release notes.
