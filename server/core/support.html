I forgot my password

On the sign-in form, choose Forgot password? and enter your email. We send a six-digit code; enter it with a new password. The code works for 15 minutes. Resetting signs out your other devices, and nothing stored on them is lost.

Do I need an account?

No. Personal works fully on one device without one, and nothing leaves that device. An account is only for syncing between devices.

My devices are not syncing

Check that both are signed in to the same email: on the phone under Review → Account, on the desktop from the sync line at the bottom of the sidebar. Sync runs by itself when the app opens, after an edit, and every few minutes while it is open. If the account screen says Sync storage full, the account has reached its 100 MB limit.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, open Account and choose Delete account. This removes the account and everything synced to it from our server straight away. What is on your devices stays there until you remove it.

Refunds

Purchases are handled by the App Store or Google Play, and so are refunds. On Apple devices, ask at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Bugs and the source code

Personal is open source. Bugs and ideas are welcome at github.com/leoner11/personal.
